The Cryptographic Existential Crisis

Classical systems like RSA and ECC rely on computational hardness—specifically the difficulty of integer factorisation.

"Shor’s 1994 algorithm reduces exponential-time complexity to polynomial-time on a large-scale quantum computer."

BB84 offers a paradigm shift: security derived from the Postulates of Quantum Mechanics, making it unconditionally secure against any computational adversary.

Heisenberg Uncertainty

Protocol Mechanics

1. Encoding (Alice)

Qubits are prepared in two mutually unbiased conjugate bases:

// Rectilinear (Z-basis)
|0⟩ → 0°, |1⟩ → 90°

// Diagonal (X-basis)
|+⟩ → 45°, |-⟩ → 135°

2. Measurement (Bob)

Bob measures in random bases. Wavefunction collapse follows the Born Rule:

P(b|ψ) = |⟨b|ψ⟩|²

Basis mismatch yields perfect 50:50 randomness, introducing the 50% sifting discard rate.
